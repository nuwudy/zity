# ZITY.IN — The Smart Local Business Platform

## 1. Vision
Empower small shops and local businesses with a free digital presence — a professional web page where they can list products, share with customers, and receive direct orders via WhatsApp.

> “Every local business deserves an online identity — without spending money or learning tech.”

---

## 2. Target Users
- Local shop owners (grocery, fashion, food, etc.)
- Freelancers and service providers (salon, tailor, mechanic, etc.)
- Home-based sellers and small-scale entrepreneurs

---

## 3. Core Features
- **Free Registration**: Instant signup (email, phone, or WhatsApp).
- **Unique URL**: `https://zity.in/yourshopname`
- **Business Web Page**:
  - Logo, address, contact, location map.
  - About section & Product gallery.
  - WhatsApp chat & social share buttons.
- **Product Listing**: Image, price, description, "Order on WhatsApp" button.
- **Smart Sharing**: WhatsApp, Instagram bio, Facebook, QR code.
- **Direct WhatsApp Orders**: Pre-filled messages (e.g., "Hi, I'd like to order 2kg of apples!").

---

## 4. Admin Dashboard
- Business approval & verification.
- Analytics (views, clicks, orders).
- Ad placements & Category management.
- Subscription management (future).

---

## 5. Monetization (Future)
- **Zity Pro Plan (₹99/year)**: Watermark removal, Priority listing, Custom domain.
- **Promoted Listings**.
- **Local Ads**.
- **Marketplace Integration**.

---

## 6. Technology Stack
- **Frontend**: Blade, TailwindCSS.
- **Backend**: Laravel 12.
- **Admin**: Filament PHP.
- **Database**: MySQL/SQLite.
